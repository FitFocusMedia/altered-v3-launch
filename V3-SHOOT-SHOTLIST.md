## 🎨 Production Notes

### Lighting Reference (Nexus Style)
Based on the Nexus Sports Nutrition analysis:
- **Dark void backgrounds** — matte black preferred
- **Single-color accent lighting** per flavour
- **Low-angle hero shots** — products look powerful
- **Neon ring/rim lighting** — dramatic edge glow
- **Water droplets** on tubs add premium feel

### Color Reference
| Flavour | Primary Accent | Hex |
|---------|---------------|-----|
| Blue Gummy Shark | Electric Cyan | #00CFFF |
| Goldy Juice | Warm Gold/Amber | #F59E0B |
| Tornado Ice Block | Red/Coral | #EF4444 |

### Brand Guidelines
- **"THE CHOSEN FEW"** — tagline, use in text overlays
- Font style: Ultra-bold, wide, all-caps
- Matte black as dominant color
- Each flavour has diagonal slash marks (////////) as decoration
- Stim levels: MID / HIGH / MAX (triple system)
